# Pay-per-call / per-lead networks for expungement traffic (researched 2026-11-20)

Only what was read on the pages below. "3P" = third-party source. "Old" = dated before 2024. If a field isn't listed, the page didn't say.

## Main finding
None of the networks checked publishes an **expungement / record-sealing** offer for publishers. The closest fit is **criminal-defense** call or lead offers. One network that buys criminal-defense leads from affiliates **explicitly excludes expungements** (Legal Brand Marketing, below). Expungement demand does exist on the attorney (buyer) side: 4LegalLeads and CounselStack sell expungement leads to lawyers. To confirm that a criminal offer accepts expungement callers, ask each network's affiliate manager in writing.

## Context: old or third-party only
- **Ringba "Pay Per Call Report" (Old, 2021):** Lead Smart listed "Criminal Lawyers Pay Per Call - 90 Seconds - Nationwide" with no payout given. No DUI or expungement offers listed. https://www.ringba.com/blog/the-pay-per-call-report-march-26-2021
- **Service Direct:** blog/press (2020, Old) says it added a Legal vertical and runs a pay-per-call affiliate program. Its core business is home services, and criminal defense is not mentioned. https://blog.servicedirect.com/service-direct-integrates-with-the-ringba-ecosystem
- **DOPPCALL (via OfferVault, 3P):** a search snippet showed legal consultation offers at $20–$100 CPA. The offer page now returns 404, and a criminal-defense offer could not be verified.

## Expungement demand on the buyer side (useful for direct deals, not publisher programs)
- **4LegalLeads** sells Criminal Defense leads to attorneys at $60–$85 per lead, with "Criminal Expungement" as a subcategory or add-on. This is the attorney's price, not a publisher payout. Its affiliate program on FlexOffers ($32 per valid registration) is marked **Closed / not currently offered** (3P). https://4legalleads.com/c-5-lightbox.html (403 when fetched, read via search snippet) ; https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/4legalleads-com-affiliate-program
- **CounselStack** sells "Criminal Expungement" leads to attorneys ("$100+"). No affiliate or publisher program mentioned. https://www.counselstack.com/legal-leads/criminal-expungement

## Checked: no publisher program found, or doesn't take this vertical
- **Marketcall:** verticals are Insurance, Finance, Home Services, Real Estate, Travel and Rehab. **No legal vertical.** https://www.marketcall.com/industries
- **Lawyer.com (FlexOffers):** 24% per lead, but marked "not currently offering this affiliate program" (updated 2025-01-24, 3P). https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/lawyer-com-affiliate-program
- **"Legal Lead Pro":** no such network found. "Legal Lead Pros" is a law-firm marketing agency with no publisher program.
- **LegalMatch, Total Attorneys, Avvo, Lawyers.com, Martindale-Nolo / Nolo leads:** all are buyer-side (they sell leads to attorneys). No public affiliate or publisher program was found. martindale.com and nolo.com returned 403.
- **Ringba / TrackDrive:** call-tracking platforms, not networks. Ringba-based networks list offers separately.
