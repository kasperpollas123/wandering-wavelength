## Dead or parked domains

- **expunge.io**, **xpunge.com** and **expungementhelp.com** all redirect to a `/lander` parking page. expunge.io goes to forsale.godaddy.com.
- **mycleanslate.com** redirects to a HugeDomains "for sale" page.
- **expungement.com** was blocked by a Cloudflare challenge or returned 503, so it could not be read.
- **LegalZoom** and **Rocket Lawyer**: no expungement product page found.

## Non-commercial partner routes (no payout)

RecordSponge (OR, https://recordsponge.com/), Spongey.app (MO) and RecordFixer recruit legal-aid and clinic partners. These are free tools to list, not affiliate income.

## Suggestion

Since no record-clearing service publishes a programme, the realistic route is direct outreach for a referral or sponsored-listing deal with Rasa (contact page), RecordGone/Higbee (877-573-7273) or EasyExpunctions (contact page). Check state bar rules first: law firms (Rasa PLLC, RecordGone, WipeRecord, Erase The Case) generally cannot pay per-referral fees to non-lawyers, so a law-firm deal would likely have to be a flat advertising fee. EasyExpunctions is not a law firm, so it is the most plausible commission partner. Checkr's $25 consumer-check payout is the only ready-made programme relevant to "check your own record".
