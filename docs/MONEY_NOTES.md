# Ways to make money from ExpungementAttorneys.org (notes, November 2026)

Saved from an outside review (Google AI Studio, Nov 20 2026) plus our own view. Nothing here is
switched on. Kasper's standing decision: no inquiries, lead forms or outreach until the site has traffic.

## Market facts we checked ourselves (Google Ads keyword data, Nov 2026)
- "expungement lawyer": about 27,100 US searches a month (seasonal: 40,500 in winter, 5,400 in Oct 2026);
  advertisers bid about $24 to $174 per click.
- "expungement attorney near me": about 720 a month, $18 to $141 per click.
- "how much does an expungement cost": about 480 a month, $9 to $46 per click.
- "expungement lawyer houston": about 140 a month, from about $32 per click.
High click prices mean law firms pay well for these visitors. Record clearing is mostly statewide paperwork,
so one firm can serve a whole state.

## Ideas from the review, and our verdict
1. **Statewide / city featured placements** (flat monthly fee, e.g. top of a state page). Suggested price
   $300-1,000 a month per state, $99-299 per big city. Verdict: best fit. Works because expungement firms serve
   whole states; flat advertising fees are the ethics-safe shape (no share of legal fees). Must be labelled
   "Sponsored". Needs traffic numbers to sell. Candidates already listed: statewide specialists such as
   Xpunge Chicago, Erase The Case, RecordGone and others.
2. **Pay-per-call networks** (a tracked phone number on a "talk to a record-clearing specialist" button;
   paid per call over ~90-120 seconds). Verdict: good first-money test once there is traffic, because it needs
   no outreach. Keep it as a separate, clearly labelled button; never replace the listed firms' own numbers.
   Payouts in the review ($50-150 per call) are not verified.
3. **Low-cost / DIY record-clearing services as affiliates** for people who earn too much for legal aid but
   can't pay $1,500+. Verdict: good fit and honest, if real programmes exist; coverage of such services is
   limited to some states. Needs checking which ones run affiliate programmes.
4. **Background-check (FCRA) case referrals** on the guide about expunged records still showing up:
   consumer-protection firms take these cases at no cost to the client. Verdict: genuine and useful to readers;
   small volume; a single labelled box on one guide. Check which firms buy such leads and the state rules.
5. **Paid "claimed profile" tier** that removes competitors from the firm's page and sorts it higher.
   Verdict: partly no. Letting firms claim and correct their listing is fine (and earns links); hiding
   competitors and paid ranking would mislead visitors on a .org information site.
6. **Eligibility quiz that routes leads to one partner firm.** Verdict: not now. Conflicts with the
   "no quiz tools / keep it simple" rule, and routing one paying firm as "the" recommendation is where
   lawyer-advertising ethics rules bite. Revisit only with a clear "advertising" setup.
- Display ads (AdSense): the review advises against them on a legal .org; we agree.

## Ethics rule to keep in mind
Lawyer advertising rules (ABA Model Rule 7.2 and state versions) bar paying a non-lawyer a share of legal fees
or paying for a recommendation. Flat advertising fees, per-call or per-inquiry marketing fees and labelled
sponsorships are the usual allowed shapes; check the state rules before any deal.

## Checked Nov 20 2026 (full reports with links in docs/money-research/)
- **Pay-per-call:** no network publishes an expungement offer. Closest: CallScaler criminal-lawyer calls
  ($40-100 per qualified call, 60-120 s), RingPartner/Buyerlink and Bark (legal category, terms unconfirmed),
  eLocal (needs a minimum monthly call volume). Legal Brand Marketing explicitly does not pay for expungement
  leads. Marketcall has no legal vertical; 4LegalLeads and Lawyer.com affiliate programmes are closed.
  Needs written confirmation that expungement calls qualify, and real traffic.
- **Record-clearing affiliates:** none of the record-clearing services (Rasa, RecordGone, EasyExpunctions,
  WipeRecord, Erase The Case...) has an affiliate programme. Only nearby: Checkr's own-record background check
  ($25 per paid individual signup via PartnerStack). Direct deals possible later: EasyExpunctions (not a law
  firm, $499 FL / $699 TX) could pay per referral; law firms (Rasa, RecordGone) only flat advertising fees.
- **FCRA referrals:** no firm runs a paid referral programme for websites. Firms such as Berger Montague pay
  for sponsored investigation pages (ClassAction.org, Top Class Actions model) - so a flat-fee sponsored box
  on our background-check guide, sold directly, is the realistic route. NACA's find-an-attorney is the free
  neutral link for readers now.
- **Ethics (sources in fcra.md):** flat sponsorship or fixed per-lead fee OK if the site doesn't recommend or
  vouch for the lawyer (ABA 7.2(b)(1) cmt 5; NYSBA Op. 1294, 2026). Not OK: fee share, pay-on-signup or
  pay-on-filing, "vetted/best lawyer" wording. NJ, FL, MI stricter.
- **Conclusion:** no plug-in revenue exists for this niche. The realistic money is direct, flat-fee
  sponsorships sold to statewide expungement firms (and FCRA firms) once traffic numbers exist.
