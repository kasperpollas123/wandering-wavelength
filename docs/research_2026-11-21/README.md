## 1. Traffic: the real bottleneck is indexing, not content
- Google Search Console, last 90 days: only 18 pages have shown up in Google at all (2 city pages, 11 attorney
  pages, home, FAQ...). 0 clicks. Chicago city page: 419 impressions for "expungement attorney chicago" at ~55.
- Sitemap: 1,573 URLs submitted. Random sample of 60 sitemap URLs (URL Inspection): 3 indexed,
  45 "Discovered - currently not indexed", 13 "unknown to Google". State pages and most guides are not indexed yet;
  the cost guide, the free-help page, home, Houston and Chicago are.
- Meaning: Google knows the pages but isn't spending crawl on them. Typical for a young site with ~1,300 similar
  attorney pages and few quality links. More pages won't help until this moves.
- Levers: (a) a few real links (see section 3), (b) strong internal links from the indexed pages (home, cost guide,
  free-help) to state pages and guides, (c) consider noindexing thin attorney pages (those with little unique text)
  so crawl goes to state/city/guide pages, (d) keep IndexNow/sitemap pings (Bing).
- Many impressions are quoted name+address lookups ("vaughn law pllc" "77002") - likely other directories/tools
  checking firms, not real searchers.

## 3. Backlinks (full plan: backlinks_plan.md; no outreach yet, by Kasper's choice)
Competitors' real links come from university law/library guides, county reentry pages, legal-help portals and
reentry nonprofits; the rest is spam. Best categories for us: county/state law library research guides, college
reentry/career pages, public library reentry pages, reentry councils, consumer-protection/tenant-screening groups.
Lead with free/data pages (free-help page, cost table, CFPB tracker), not the firm list. Press angle: the CFPB
complaints tracker (HireRight complaints ~30/yr -> ~90 in 2025 was already news in Feb 2026) - publish a method
note + CSV first.

## 4. Money (full report: docs/money-research/new_angles_2026-11.md)
New verified options: Optery (people-search removal) 30% for 12 months, 90-day cookie - fits the background-check
guide; paid enhanced firm profiles (Super Lawyers charges $75-400/mo, Justia Pro $19.99/mo) if labelled and not
affecting ranking; DeleteMe 30-40%; Intoxalock $10/lead-$50/install for DUI guides; credit builders (Kikoff $33,
Self $12, Chime $10.80; careful wording). Dead ends: legal marketplaces (no publisher programs), FBI channelers,
job boards, grants (Ad Grants needs a 501(c)(3) and bars affiliate sites).
